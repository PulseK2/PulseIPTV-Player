Pulse IPTV — Next-Generation IPTV Desktop Player for Windows


The Ultimate All-in-One IPTV Streaming Experience

Pulse IPTV is a high-performance Windows desktop media player designed from the ground up for modern streaming. Built with a native Direct3D11 hardware-accelerated playback engine, Pulse IPTV eliminates the buffering, sluggish channel zapping, and bloated interfaces common to older players. Whether you're watching live 4K sports, catching up on breaking news, or binge-watching full multi-season series, Pulse IPTV delivers silky-smooth playback with vibrant colors and instant responsiveness.

Engineered for seamless compatibility with Xtream Codes APIs, M3U/M3U8 playlists, and local files, Pulse IPTV automatically organizes tens of thousands of channels, movies, and shows into a clean, searchable media hub. With built-in EPG schedule caching, instant favorites, background VOD downloads, and intelligent server failover, it’s the only player you'll ever need.




⚡ Direct3D11 Hardware-Accelerated Engine
4K UHD & HDR Ready: Leverages your GPU (NVIDIA, AMD, Intel) for crystal-clear 4K/60fps playback with true Rec.2020 HDR and Dolby/AC-3 audio support.
Instant Channel Zapping: Enjoy ultra-low latency MPEG-TS (.ts) streaming with instant channel switching or toggle to adaptive HLS (.m3u8) with a single click.
Smart Buffering Engine: Adaptive caching buffers ahead to prevent stuttering and dropouts on congested networks.

📺 Live TV & Integrated Electronic Program Guide (EPG)
Real-Time TV Guide: See what's playing now and what's on next with live timeline progress bars for every channel.
Offline EPG Caching: Compresses and stores channel schedules locally so you never have to wait for tens of thousands of listings to redownload.
In-Player Mini Guide: Browse upcoming shows and switch channels directly underneath the active video stream without interrupting your viewing.

🎬 Full On-Demand Cinema & Series Hub
Movies (VOD): Stream high-definition movies with cover art, release years, plot summaries, and smooth time-scrubbing.
Series Browser: Explore TV shows with dedicated season pickers, episode descriptions, and thumbnail previews.
Offline Downloads: Download your favorite movies and series directly to your PC for offline viewing when traveling or without internet.

🛡️ Bulletproof Connectivity & Smart Server Failover
One-Click Auto-Fill: Paste any Xtream Codes URL, M3U link, or credentials—our smart parser detects and configures your account instantly.
Multi-Server Failover: Add backup server addresses; if your primary provider server ever goes offline or experiences downtime, Pulse IPTV automatically switches servers in the background with zero interruption.
Multi-Account Manager: Save and switch between multiple IPTV provider subscriptions effortlessly.

🎨 Modern, Intuitive User Interface
Sleek Dark Theme: Clean obsidian and electric-cyan aesthetic designed for high-end gaming and desktop monitors.
One-Click Favorites: Star your top live channels, movies, and series to access them in an instant.
Custom Aspect Ratios: Cycle on-the-fly between Default, 16:9, 4:3, and 21:9 UltraWide modes.
Theater & Fullscreen Mode: Double-click or press F / ESC to jump into distraction-free fullscreen entertainment.
